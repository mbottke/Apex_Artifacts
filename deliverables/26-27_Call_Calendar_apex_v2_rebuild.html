Resident On-Call Schedule

The academic year comprises 354 on-call days, each staffed by one intern and one senior resident. Assignments are distributed so that, within each postgraduate class, the on-call workload is shared as evenly as the rotation requirements permit. Select a resident to review their assignments and individual workload summary.
