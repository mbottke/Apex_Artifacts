§ A

Two delivered versions

Two deliverables follow from one diagnosis, and they answer different questions. One asks “what is the smallest change that makes the draft legal?” The other asks “what is the fairest legal schedule we can build?” Both are complete, and both pass the same machine audit.

Version 1
Minimal Repair
  • Fixes only the rule violations — nothing else moves.
  • Each fix is a 1-for-1 swap on the same day of the week.
  • Every resident keeps their exact totals and day-mix.
  • Best for adopting now with minimal review.
Version 2
Full Rebuild
  • Re-derives the entire year via CP-SAT optimization.
  • Balanced so the on-call burden is as level as the rules allow.
  • Totals re-balanced to near-equal within each class.
  • Best for a fresh, provably-balanced calendar.

Why the repair is invisible on the tally tabs

Every swap trades two residents who are both on call on the same weekday. Trading one Friday for another leaves each resident's count of Mondays, Tuesdays … Sundays untouched — a permutation that preserves every marginal distribution. So the workbook's year-to-date totals are numerically identical before and after: twenty conflicts vanish without moving a single tally, and no new soft conflict or back-to-back call is introduced.

§ C

Version 2 — Full Rebuild

The rebuild re-derives the year from the rules up, then makes it as even as those rules allow.

Rules enforced as hard constraints

  • No resident on call during a no-call rotation (Away, NICU, Peds Inpatient, Peds ED, Leave).
  • Rotation day-restrictions: GYN/CM no Sun/Mon/Wed · Ultrasound no Sun/Wed · Cardiology no Tue · Dermatology no Mon · FM-Ambulatory (2nd half) no Sun/Mon/Wed · R2-Pediatrics no Thu.
  • No CBP or R3-Orthopedics call at all — even the “limited/soft” uses are eliminated.
  • PTO windows and the 9/10 Nexplanon date fully respected.
  • Night-float weeknights fixed by the rotation grid; no back-to-back call (post-call day) for anyone.
  • R3 take no Sunday senior call (single Lux-style exception permitted).
  • R2 carry 7–8 Sundays and 14–16 weekend (Fri+Sat+Sun) nights each.
  • Holidays: ≤ one major or two minor per senior; the four “mix-up” holidays on four different interns; interns cover Thanksgiving, Christmas & New Year's.
  • Coverage: one intern + one senior every night, including the 12/20–1/2 winter block.

The fairness objective  Within each class the solver minimizes the inequality of every weekday's load and of the total weighted-inconvenience load, where each call is weighted by how unwelcome its day is:

weight   Wed, Thu = 1  ·  Mon, Tue = 2  ·  Fri, Sun = 3  ·  Sat = 4
The schedule is then made as even as the rules allow — lower spread is fairer.

§ D

Three deliberate structural decisions

Three choices in the rebuild are intentional rather than incidental — worth stating plainly.

Winter coverage Interns are added to the 12/20–1/2 holiday block so Christmas and New Year's have intern call — per the rule that interns schedule Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's. The preliminary had seniors covering solo.
Patel preserved Patel keeps his 12 early-year “second-senior” backup days and elevated total (35–36 vs the class's 33), reflecting his off-cycle volume. Ten original dates are kept exactly; the two that were themselves rule violations — 9/10 (Nexplanon) and 9/27 (GYN/CM Sunday) — are relocated to other Patel-eligible July–September weekdays. He is the single intentional outlier; his backup-day partner rotates across the class.
8/20 restored The preliminary's missing intern on 8/20 (Hanna Hard's night-float block) is filled.
§ E

Recommendations

Independent of which version you adopt, six actions carry the most value — roughly in priority order.

  1. Fix the five no-call-rotation errors first — Bower 7/17, Mautino 10/30 & 11/13, Sublette 4/14, Bottke 5/17. Highest-risk: a resident physically unavailable for call.
  2. Add intern coverage to the winter holidays (Christmas, New Year's) — currently senior-only.
  3. Even out holidays. Stanek (3) and Beutel (3 minor) are over; Hard, Strand, Bower, Sublette are under. Put the four “mix-up” holidays on four different interns.
  4. Relieve Mautino — in the preliminary he carries the heaviest senior load (9 Fridays + 6 Saturdays).
  5. Respect post-call on the four back-to-back pairs, especially the two holiday-weekend ones.
  6. Add a block-boundary check. Several conflicts fall on the first/last day of a 2-week rotation block (e.g., 9/27, 2/28) — worth a standing check whenever a rotation changes mid-weekend.
§ F

Alternatives considered

  • Pure fairnessRejected
    Letting R3s share Sundays makes every senior's day-mix identical — the tightest possible balance — but it breaks the R3-no-Sunday rule, so it was not used.
  • Preserve the R2-Sunday / R3-Friday splitAdopted
    Slightly less uniform across classes but faithful to program structure. This is the Version 2 you have.
  • Minimal vs full
    Version 1 if you want the smallest change that is rule-clean; Version 2 if you want the fairest calendar. Both are delivered, so the choice stays open.
Workbook tracking tables — both Excel versions

Both workbooks keep the program's original live formulas — the monthly per-resident COUNTIF totals, the “Day of Weeks Tracking” tables, and the YTD cross-month SUM rollups. None were replaced with static numbers. Because the schedule changed, every formula's cached value was recomputed and written back — so the tables read correctly on open — and then verified cell-by-cell against the solved schedule: all 17 residents' totals and all five day-of-week groups match, on every month tab and the YTD tab.
